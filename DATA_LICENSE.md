# Dataset license and attribution

The Online Retail II workbook and the small transaction sample in this repository are dataset-derived material, not original project code. UCI lists Online Retail II under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

**Attribution:** Chen, Daqing. (2012). *Online Retail II* [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D.

UCI dataset page: <https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii>  
License text: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

The tracked sample is a deterministic, month-stratified selection of whole invoices from the clean dataset. It retains invoice lines and includes project cleaning flags. The stock-code audit is a derived summary of codes, row counts, line revenue, and common descriptions, and is also subject to the dataset's CC BY 4.0 attribution terms. The downloaded workbook and full raw/processed CSVs are excluded from Git.

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to the original project code and documentation. It does not replace or override the UCI dataset license for the workbook or sample data.
